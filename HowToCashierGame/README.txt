CONTROLS:
	INITIAL GAME LOAD:
		WASD for movements
			W -> Forward
			S -> Backwards
			A -> Left
			D -> Right
	

	TUTORIAL DIALOUGE VIEW:
		There will be a text box that begins tellin you how to play the game and interact with objects
		To progress through these, once the text on that boxz has finished being displayed press 'E' to move on to the next box
		Once all boxes have gone through they will Dissappear
		This will later be added to the main scene and act for as a tutorial type system

	INITIAL STORE STATE:
		When you first load into the store you are in a first-person freeroam camera view. You can move around the store with WASD as stated before
		To get to the register view to begin the day you need to walk around the the back of the register
		As you approach the back you will see the top of the register go green.
		Once its green that means you are close enough to interact with it by clicking
		Once you click you will be transported to the register view. This will be talked about in the next section

	MAIN REGISTRER VIEW:
		Initailly you will be looking forwards at the customer
		This customer will place thier items on the belt to your right
		To interact with these items you need to click and HOLD them, if you let go you will drop the item
		If the item drops of the conveyor it will respone at the beginning
		Once you are holding the item, simply drag it over the scanning area. 
		This Scanning area is located where the display for the total price is located
		Once the items are dragged over the scanner thier price will be added to the total and displayed
		Drop these items to the left of the scanning area in the slight highlighted  holding station
		Once all items have been scanned, if you hover your move over this slightly highlighted area, it will becomed a highlighted green
		If you then click on this green area it will change your camera view to the bagging station.

	BAGGING VIEW:
		In this view you will see the scanned items (and unscanned if you missed the scanner)
		You can then click and hold on the items again to pick it up and drap them onto the "bag", this is the grey brick to your left
		Once released on the brick the item will be bagged
		Its important to note it will NOT be bagged if you placed the item in the holding area without scanning it
		If you wish to then return to the register view then move your muse to the bottom right where there now now a new slightly highlighted area
		Once this area is hovered over it will turn a slight green like before
		Click on this are and you will return to the register view
