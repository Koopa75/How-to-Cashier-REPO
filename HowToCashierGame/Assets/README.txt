CONTROLS:
	INITIAL GAME LOAD:
		WASD for movements
			W -> Forward
			S -> Backwards
			A -> Left
			D -> Right
		CLICK LEFT MOUSE BUTTON TO INTERACT WITH ITEMS
	

	MAIN MENU
		This will be the first thing you see when you boot up the game. There will be two buttons "New Game" and "Load Game"
		There is no save system as of now so that button does nothing, but if you click the New Game button it will send you to the tutorial scene
		Which we will talk about now.
	
	TUTORIAL DIALOUGE VIEW:
		There will be a text box with our Mascot "Mr. Cent" that begins telling you how to play the game and interact with objects
		There will also be videos to accompany some of the explinations
		To progress through these, once the text on that box has finished being displayed press 'E' to move on to the next box
		Once all boxes have gone through Mr. Cent will bid you well and send you to the main game

	INITIAL STORE STATE:
		When you first load into the store you are in a first-person freeroam camera view. You can move around the store with WASD as stated before
		To get to the register view to begin the day you need to walk around the the back of the register
		As you approach the back you will see the top of the register go green.
		Once its green that means you are close enough to interact with it by clicking
		Once you click you will be transported to the register view. This will be talked about in the next section

	MAIN REGISTRER VIEW:
		Initailly you will be looking forwards at the customer
		This customer will place thier items on the belt to your right
		To interact with these items you need to click and HOLD them, if you let go you will drop the item
		If the item drops of the conveyor it will respone at the beginning
		Once you are holding the item, simply drag it over the scanning area. 
		This Scanning area is located where the display for the total price is located
		Once the items are dragged over the scanner thier price will be added to the total and displayed
		Drop these items to the left of the scanning area in the slight highlighted  holding station
		Once all items have been scanned, if you hover your move over this slightly highlighted area, it will become a highlighted green
		If you then click on this green area it will change your camera view to the bagging station.

	BAGGING VIEW:
		In this view you will see the scanned items (and unscanned if you missed the scanner)
		You can then click and hold on the items again to pick it up and drap them onto the "bag", this is the grey basket to your left
		Once released on the basket the item will be bagged
		Its important to note it will NOT be bagged if you placed the item in the holding area without scanning it first
		If you wish to then return to the register view then move your mouse to the bottom right where there is now a new slightly highlighted area
		Once this area is hovered over it will turn a slight green like before
		Click on this area and you will return to the register view


	CHECKING OUT CUSTOMERS:
		Once all items have been bagged you can click ther "checkout" button above the register. Once pressed the current customer will leave
		and another customer will walk up and take there place, there item will also spawn on the belt.
		You can see how many customers you have checked out by pressing 'TAB' to open the menu on the right then go to 'Player Info' and you will see it
		A customer will alwasy be at the register. If you check out the last one a new one wil instanlty spawn in


	THE DAY SYSTEM:
		Eventually there will be an entire week, but for now its just one day
		But once you interact with the register and start checking out customers the timer in the top left of the screen will begin counting down
		Once this timer hits zero the day will end and you will have two choices
		Return to the main menu and save your progress or continue to the next day.


SETUP:
	There is not much setup that is needed for this game at all.
	Just start the game and it will see that it is your first time playing and send you the tutorial scene like mentioned before
	I guess the only setup you might need will come from the settings where the will be things you can enable to change like sound volume, resolution, shader, etc.
	But there is nothing needed for set up
	To Set up the game itself just open the build folder and click the executable file in there to open it, and once that's done it will open the game
